Printer alignment

Labels download as a PDF laid out for Avery Presta 94749 (45 per Letter sheet, 3 across × 15 down). Open the PDF and print it with scale set to 100% / Actual size — not "Fit to page", which shrinks the sheet so labels drift further off toward the bottom.

  1. Download the alignment test and print it on plain paper.
  2. With a ruler, measure from the top edge of label #1 to the top edge of label #43 (first label of the bottom row). On an exact print it's 233.4 mm. If yours is different, enter your measurement below — the labels are stretched to match your printer.
  3. Hold the test over a label sheet against a window. If everything is shifted by the same amount, use Move right / Move down (negative numbers move left / up).
